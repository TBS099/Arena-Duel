Changes made: the original 128 px vector-style tiles were downscaled to 16 px
and reduced to a shared 12-colour palette with hard alpha edges.

Sheet: desert_tiles_16px.png (8 columns x 2 rows, 16x16 cells)
Tile n sits at row (n-1)//8, column (n-1)%8.

Roles:
 1 top-left cap          2 top                  3 top-right cap
 4 left edge             5 fill                 6 right edge
 7 top variant           8 top/right variant
 9 fill                 10 fill                11 top-right inner
12 left bottom edge     13 right bottom edge
14 thin floating platform, left
15 thin floating platform, middle
16 thin floating platform, right
(14 to 16 are 16x12 and top-aligned.)

Best use: thin floating platforms from tiles 14, 15 and 16.
Thick blocks (1 to 6, 12 and 13) show seams.
